D2. Problem description

116 thermal incidents explained among 180,000 units in the field. Traceon traced 116 thermal incidents on the VR-48 pack to Defective cell batch: Supplier B CB-B-0917 (116 incidents, 1668x the baseline rate; L-409 (plant P2, built 2026-10-02 to 2026-10-04, serials SN-88400 to SN-89779)). Together they account for 92.8% of all thermal claims.

D3. Containment

Quarantine of 1,380 units (cell batch CB-B-0917), instead of recalling all 180,000 units. Cost $34,500 against $4,500,000 for a blanket recall. Without containment, about 200 more incidents are projected in 30 days (90% range 170 to 233).
